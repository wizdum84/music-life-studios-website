Beat names and session names do not have to be identical. For Pro Tools, the report uses three evidence levels:

- **High confidence:** the beat name appears in a bounced or source-audio filename and is also supported by the session metadata or folder.
- **Medium confidence:** the beat name appears in the session metadata or its surrounding project folder, but the audio filenames are generic.
- **No match:** the session contains no reliable beat-name clue. It should be reviewed manually rather than guessed.
