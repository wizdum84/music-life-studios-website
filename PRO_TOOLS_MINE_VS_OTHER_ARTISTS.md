## Method note

This is a filename and folder crossmatch. It checks the audio folders inside each live Pro Tools session, excludes backups and recovery copies, and compares meaningful audio roots with production-library MP3/WAV and FL Studio/Ableton names. It does not listen to the audio or decode proprietary `.ptx` session metadata.
